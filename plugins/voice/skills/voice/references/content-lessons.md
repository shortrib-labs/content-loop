# Content Lessons

Lessons the author has taught by correcting drafts, one entry per correction
class. Every lesson applies to every draft. Entries are added only through the
`lessons` skill, and only after the author approves them.

Format per entry:

```
## L<N>. <short name>
- **Category:** tone | structure | vocabulary | hooks | evidence
- **Lesson:** <what to do or never do>
- **Taught:** <date> — <one line on the correction that taught it>
```
